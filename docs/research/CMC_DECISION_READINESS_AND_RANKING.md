# CMC decision readiness and separate ranking research

This recovery phase enables no production rules. `CMC_MODE=decision` alone is
insufficient: one unambiguous activation must name the exact rule ID, version and
specification hash, provide source-contract, causal evaluation, prospective/OOS,
tests, compatibility, baseline and owner-approval references, attest no material
negative regression, and be within its approved time window. OFF/COLLECT/SHADOW
cannot apply that authority. Rejected candidates, earlier public ownership,
invalidation, RR, confirmation and quality gates remain canonical CCI decisions.

Recommend `adverse_regime/1` as the first research candidate for eventual active
DECISION because its global inputs have captured source verification and its
action is a bounded priority decrease. Do not activate it yet. Its present
CONFIRMED behavior can only change delivery order within identical quality/RR/
technical/opportunity ranks. All eligible CONFIRMED candidates still pass through
the existing delivery path, so a priority adjustment alone demonstrates neither
a membership change nor improved economics. A rule with no measurable selection
leverage should remain SHADOW even when source data are healthy.

## Prospective evidence contract

Collect one row per original setup/economic-plan version and selection boundary:
Runtime epoch and origin IDs, scan/iteration ID, symbol/direction/mode, analysis
cutoff, selection cutoff, immutable plan ID, original canonical eligibility and
four-component rank, snapshot/feature/provenance references, source/acquisition
times, evidence states, exact rule hashes, recommendations, hypothetical/applied
actions, baseline/selected/order IDs, and observed Telegram attempt status. Link
policy batches, selection intents and applications rather than inferring one from
another. Preserve repeat boundaries but deduplicate economic plans for outcomes.

Count the following separately: CMC_DATA_COLLECTED, CMC_FEATURE_VERIFIED,
CMC_CANDIDATE_EVALUATED, CMC_RULE_TRIGGERED,
CMC_HYPOTHETICAL_SELECTION_CHANGED, CMC_RULE_ENFORCED,
PUBLIC_DELIVERY_ACTUALLY_CHANGED, VERIFIED_ECONOMIC_OUTCOME. The recovery adds
exact-rank leverage counters to selection intents. Actual tie frequency remains
BLOCKED_EXTERNAL until an owner-approved prospective cohort is supplied.

Define the decision rule and evaluation plan before observing outcome labels.
Use contiguous time/regime splits, grouped economic plans, explicit acquisition
cutoffs and mature outcomes. Report sample sizes, missing/stale proportions,
trigger rates, forgone winners/losers, entry activation, fees/slippage assumptions,
net opportunity R, tail loss/drawdown and uncertainty by regime and direction.
Select sample size from a declared minimum detectable effect and dependence-aware
power analysis; no arbitrary observation count proves expectancy. Retain an untouched
prospective/OOS set and compare the identical canonical candidate population.
No shadow send is a filled trade, and a deterministic fixture is not economic evidence.

## Secondary ranking research specification — no implementation authority

The current mechanisms do not establish a need to change ranking. If prospective
evidence shows too few exact ties, test this separate, pre-registered hypothesis
offline. Scope is new, already eligible CONFIRMED plans only; rejected candidates,
prior-public follow-ups, lifecycle progression, economic geometry, public ownership,
quality grades, RR and all canonical hard gates remain unchanged.

For an explicit research capacity K in {1,3,5}, define Q as original quality score,
R as original planned RR, T as original technical score, O as original opportunity
score and H as a verified, causal, versioned adverse-regime priority in {-1,0}.
Define the proposed lexicographic research key:

`(grade_order, -floor(Q/2), -floor(R/0.25), -H, -Q, -R, -T, -O, canonical_major_rank, symbol, economic_plan_id)`.

This explicitly changes authority within narrow Q/R bands and is a new research
policy, not a reinterpretation of current exact-rank ties. Missing or stale H is
zero. K and the bin widths are research parameters to freeze before outcome review;
searching many alternatives requires multiplicity controls and new holdout data.
Compare current exact-rank ordering, canonical selection and proposed top-K on
identical populations. Measure how often selected membership changes before claiming
any economic effect. V1's unrestricted CONFIRMED delivery has no such K cap; a cap
would need an independent product/safety review and explicit owner approval.

Required evidence includes independent source semantics, zero look-ahead, preserved
quality/confirmation/RR gates, stable results across time/regimes, adequate statistical
power, prospective net benefit after costs, no material tail-risk regression and a
tested immediate OFF rollback. A source/storage/activation failure restores canonical
selection. No ranking, capacity or new veto authority may deploy under the approval
of an older rule: approve the exact new rule version/specification hash, source
contract, evaluation design, rollout budget and deployment separately.
